About Hydro Electric

A small plumbing and electrical business, run by its owner. When you call, you speak to the person who'll do or oversee the work.

Who you'll be dealing with

Hydro Electric (Pty) Ltd is run by Kabir (Kas). The business previously traded as Izibulo Electrical and now does both plumbing and electrical work as Hydro Electric.

The aim is simple: give a fair written quote, turn up when promised, do the work properly, and leave the place clean.

Qualifications and registration

  • Qualified plumbers and electricians
  • Electrical Certificates of Compliance (COCs) issued
  • Registered electrical business
  • B-BBEE Level 1 Contributor: 135% procurement recognition
  • Company registration number: 2026/822005/07

What you can expect

A written quote first

You know the price before work starts. If something unexpected comes up, we check with you before going ahead.

Plain explanations

We tell you what's wrong and what the options are, without jargon.

Tested before we leave

Electrical work is tested and certified where required. Plumbing is checked for leaks and pressure.

A clean site

We clean up after ourselves before we leave.

Tell us what needs fixing

Send a WhatsApp with a photo of the problem, or give us a call. We'll come back to you with a written quote.
