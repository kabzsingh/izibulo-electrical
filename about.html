Our story

Built on referrals, not adverts

Izibulo Electrical started as a one-van operation in 2011. Today we're a small, tightly-run team of qualified electricians who still answer the phone ourselves.

Why "Izibulo"

Izibulo means "the firstborn" — a nod to founder Themba Nkosi's first business, and to doing the fundamentals properly before anything fancy. That philosophy still shapes how every job is run: safety and paperwork first, then the finish.

How we work

We quote in writing, show up when we say we will, and don't consider a job finished until you have a signed Certificate of Compliance in hand. No shortcuts on earthing, no verbal-only sign-offs.
