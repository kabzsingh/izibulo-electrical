Electrical Certificates of Compliance

An electrical COC confirms that a property's electrical installation is safe and meets the regulations. You'll usually need one when:

  • You sell a property
  • New electrical work is done or the installation is changed
  • A landlord, tenant or insurer asks for one

How a COC inspection works

We inspect and test the installation and tell you what, if anything, needs fixing. You get a written quote for any repairs. Once everything passes, we issue the certificate.
