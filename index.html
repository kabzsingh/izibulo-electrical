Why one team for both

Water and electricity meet in more places than people expect: geysers, pool pumps, borehole pumps, dishwashers and washing machines. When one team understands both, a burst geyser gets fixed in one visit, without waiting for a second contractor.

Selling your house?

You'll need a valid electrical Certificate of Compliance before transfer. We can inspect, fix what's needed and issue the COC.
